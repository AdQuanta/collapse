   *Dimensional Scaling Drives Monotonic Polar Score Convergence and Unit Full-Sphere Coverage.*

5. **Dominant Panel**:
   Panel (a), illustrating the steady monotonic upward trajectory of $S_{\mathrm{B}}$ with increasing detector Hilbert space dimension $d = 2^N$.

6. **Instant Comparison**:
   The monotonic upward progression of structured spin-ring scores ($0.192 \to 0.807$) contrasted against the invariant flat horizontal baseline of the Haar ensemble ($S_{\mathrm{B}} = 0$).

   Median $S_{\mathrm{B}}$ progression: $0.192$ ($N=11$), $0.341$ ($N=12$), $0.495$ ($N=13$), $0.638$ ($N=14$), $0.742$ ($N=15$), $0.807$ ($N=16$); coverage is exactly $1.000$ across all rows; $|c_2| \le 0.25$ throughout.

8. **Alternative Explanation Addressed**:
   Rules out the possibility that high polar scores are an artifact of meridional pinching, radial concentration, or unpopulated regions on the sphere; full coverage and low harmonic distortion are simultaneously preserved.

9. **Why Main Text vs. SI**:
   Provides the multi-size, multi-temporal robustness evidence required to elevate a single-instance numerical observation into a verified scaling trend.
