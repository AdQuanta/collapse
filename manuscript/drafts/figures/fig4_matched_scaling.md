# Figure Card 4: Matched-Ring Finite-Size Trend

## Figure specification

1. **Claim-like title:** *The matched sequence shows a monotonic finite-size trend toward a Born-like polar profile.*
2. **One-sentence takeaway:** Across four deterministic saved times for each $N=11,\ldots,16$, the median polar score rises from $0.192$ to $0.807$, while every matched row retains unit polar coverage and satisfies the declared second-harmonic gate.
3. **Question answered:** Is the highlighted $N=16$ map isolated, or does it belong to a systematic trend across the audited matched sequence?
4. **Panel flow:** Individual time-slice scores, median, and maximum versus $N$ → median $|c_2|$ and polar coverage versus $N$.
5. **Dominant panel:** The six-size polar-score panel, with deterministic time points visible and the median emphasized.
6. **Instant comparison:** Matched-ring scores versus the analytical Haar one-point-intensity baseline $S_{\mathrm B}=0$.
7. **Quantitative anchors:** Median $S_{\mathrm B}=(0.192,0.411,0.571,0.715,0.788,0.807)$ for $N=11,\ldots,16$; 24 rows; unit polar coverage; $|c_2|\le0.25$.
8. **Alternative interpretation addressed:** Demonstrates that the $N=16$ diagnostic is part of an ordered finite-size sequence, while explicitly refusing a thermodynamic extrapolation. The second harmonic is a limited azimuthal check, not proof of full azimuthal uniformity.
9. **Why main text:** It is the only audited multi-size trend and provides the main robustness evidence for the numerical observation.

## Draft caption

> **FIG. 4. Matched-ring finite-size trend and structure versus the Haar null.** (a) Polar score $S_{\mathrm B}$ for four deterministic saved times at each $N=11,\ldots,16$, with the median and maximum highlighted. The median rises monotonically from $0.192$ at $N=11$ to $0.807$ at $N=16$. The dotted $S_{\mathrm B}=0$ line is the exact Haar ensemble one-point-intensity baseline, not a finite-$d$ sample mean. (b) Polar coverage is unity and the second azimuthal harmonic remains below the declared $|c_2|\le0.25$ gate for all 24 matched rows. Lines guide the eye. The saved times are deterministic observations, so the figure supplies neither stochastic error bars nor a thermodynamic extrapolation.
