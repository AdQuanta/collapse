## Draft caption

> **FIG. 2. Exact projective construction and numerical bridge.** Writing the qubit-first unitary as $U=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\C&D\end{pmatrix}$, the homogeneous pencils $(\alpha C+\beta D)\eta=0$ and $(\alpha A+\beta B)\eta=0$ select product inputs that reach qubit poles $0$ and $1$, respectively. For regular pencils, each label has $d$ projective roots counted with algebraic multiplicity. Jacobi complementary-minor duality forces the two labelled multisets to be exact Bloch antipodes. The production calculation instead solves $Cv=\lambda Av$, enumerating separable outputs of $U(t)$; these are forward pole preimages under $U^\dagger(t)=U(-t)$. Roots at infinity and singular pencils require the homogeneous analysis described in the Supplemental Material.
