# Manuscript Drafting Workspace

This directory contains the editable architecture, figure cards, and section drafts for the Letter. The active story is Narrative D from `manuscript/NARRATIVE_REGISTRY.md`: exact projective outcome geometry, an exact Haar isotropic null, and a finite matched-ring root-count diagnostic. It does not claim an operational Born probability law or a theory of stable records.

## Source-of-truth order

When sources disagree, use this order:

1. `manuscript/EVIDENCE_REGISTRY.md` for claim status and wording ceilings.
2. `manuscript/NUMERICAL_PROVENANCE.md` and `manuscript/audits/` for traceability and limitations.
3. `manuscript/NARRATIVE_REGISTRY.md` for the selected story and rejected alternatives.
4. `manuscript/main.tex` and `manuscript/supplement.tex` for current notation and equation conventions.
5. Files in this directory as editable working prose and planning documents.

## Working set

- `architecture/pitch.md`: territory, gap, contribution, findings, significance, and boundary.
- `architecture/claim_ladder.md`: evidence-linked claims with explicit wording ceilings.
- `architecture/figure_plan.md`: four-figure narrative and the logical transition between figures.
- `figures/`: one figure card and draft caption per main-text figure.
- `sections/`: abstract, introduction, results, and discussion drafts.

## Integrity rules

- Treat root existence, root geometry, operational probability, outcome selection, and record formation as distinct questions.
- Call the two outcome sets exact antipodal partners, not independent clouds.
- State the Haar result as an ensemble one-point theorem, not finite-realization concentration.
- Describe the matched-ring data as a finite-size trend over deterministic saved times, not thermodynamic convergence or stochastic sampling.
- Reserve full-sphere occupancy for the audited $N=16,t=10^4$, $36\times18$ map. The scaling figure reports polar coverage and the second azimuthal harmonic separately.
- Preserve the production/forward bridge: production roots at $+t$ are forward pole preimages for $U(-t)$; for the real highlighted Hamiltonian, the same-time forward map reflects azimuth while preserving the polar diagnostics.
- Keep unresolved work explicit: a preparation/selection measure, a common detector-ready macrostate, stable records, homogeneous-QZ validation, controlled asymptotics, and a mechanism for the matched-field condition.

## Revision workflow

Revise in this order: scientific claims, narrative sequence, paragraph structure, sentence quality, notation and typography, then an outsider read of the abstract and figures. No sentence may exceed the wording ceiling in `architecture/claim_ladder.md`.
