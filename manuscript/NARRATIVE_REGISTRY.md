# Narrative registry

Four candidate Letters were tested against the exact theory, the July 1, 2026
numerical cutoff, and the operational measurement objection.

| Narrative | Central claim | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| A. Unitary derivation of collapse | Special boundary states solve the measurement problem. | Rejected.  Existence of boundary-compatible states does not provide a preparation law, outcome selection, or stable records. |
| B. Emergent Born rule from generic chaos | Large generic detectors produce Born probabilities. | Rejected.  Haar dynamics is exactly isotropic in the ensemble and the eligible data do not isolate chaos or convergence. |
| C. Full measurement model for a matched spin ring | The audited ring realizes operational measurement. | Rejected.  The current numerics count projective roots requiring different detector microstates and do not test record stability. |
| D. Exact projective geometry plus a structured many-body diagnostic | Unitarity fixes an antipodal projective-root geometry; Haar is an exact isotropic null; one matched post-cutoff ring develops a dipole-dominated root-count asymmetry. | Selected.  This is the strongest coherent claim supported by both theorem and traceable data. |

## Selected Letter spine

1. Place measurement beside established subsystem arrows of time, while marking
   the stronger global-record step as unresolved.
2. Construct pole-compatible product inputs as homogeneous matrix-pencil roots.
3. Prove exact complementary-minor antipodality of the outcome root sets.
4. Derive the Haar spherical-ensemble null, which rules out axis selection by
   generic Haar scrambling.
5. Present the post-cutoff matched-ring full-sphere asymmetry and finite-size
   trend as geometric evidence only.
6. End at the operational boundary: root existence and root counts are not a
   selection measure or a record theorem.

## Claims deliberately weakened

- "Born probabilities" became "Born-like root-count geometry."
- "two independently generated outcome clouds" became "one root cloud and its
  exact antipodal outcome partner."
- "production forward roots of U(t)" became "fixed-pole separable outputs of
  U(t), equivalently forward preimages under U(-t)."
- "measurement arrow derived" became "measurement arrow motivates a restricted
  state classification problem."
