# von Neumann 1932 — Mathematical Foundations of Quantum Mechanics

**Reference**: John von Neumann, 'Mathematische Grundlagen der Quantenmechanik' [Mathematical Foundations of Quantum Mechanics], Springer, Berlin (1932). English translation: Princeton University Press (1955). ISBN: 978-0691028934

## Core Thesis
First rigorous mathematical axiomatization of quantum mechanics. Distinguished two types of evolution: Process 1 (Non-unitary, discontinuous 'collapse' upon measurement) and Process 2 (Unitary, continuous Schrödinger evolution).

## Key Concepts
- Mathematical axiomatization of quantum mechanics.
- Process 1: Non-unitary, discontinuous 'collapse' upon measurement.
- Process 2: Unitary, continuous Schrödinger evolution.
- The 'measurement problem' is fundamentally the question of why and when Process 1 occurs.
- Contains von Neumann's (flawed) no-hidden-variables proof.

## Relevance to `unitary-collapse`
Von Neumann's Process 1/Process 2 distinction is THE measurement problem that unitary-collapse aims to dissolve. The program argues Process 1 is an effective description of Process 2 acting on restricted initial states.
