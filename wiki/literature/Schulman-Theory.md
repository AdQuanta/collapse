# Schulman: Special State Theory

## Core Thesis
The Schrödinger equation always holds (pure unitarity), but the set of physically realized states is restricted to a rare subset called **"Special States"**.

## Key Mechanisms
- **Avoiding Grotesque Superpositions**: Only special states are prepared/realized, which prevents the emergence of macroscopically distinct superpositions (like Schrödinger's cat).
- **The "Kick" Mechanism**: Transitions into special states require a "proximate cause" (environmental fluctuations).
- **Cauchy Distribution**: To recover the **Born probabilities**, the distribution of these environmental "kicks" must follow a Cauchy distribution (long-tailed).
- **Future Boundary Conditions**: Proposes that the selection of special states is constrained by boundary conditions at the end of the universe.

## Relevance to `collapse`
- **Foundational Loophole**: This is the primary conceptual precursor to the `collapse` project.
- **Born Recovery**: Provides a possible mechanism (Cauchy-distributed noise) for recovering Born statistics from a restricted state-set.
