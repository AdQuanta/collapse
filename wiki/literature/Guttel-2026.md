# Guttel et al. (2026): Dynamical Transitions in Monitored Qubits

## Core Thesis
The transition from coherent quantum evolution to measurement-dominated behavior occurs via a **cascade of three sharp dynamical transitions** rather than a gradual crossover.

## Key Transitions
1. **Quantum Jump Regime ($\lambda_1 \approx 0.99$)**: Coherent Rabi oscillations halt; system begins executing continuous quantum jumps.
2. **State-Freezing Regime ($\lambda_2 \approx 0.92$)**: The system becomes trapped near a stable eigenstate.
3. **Quantum Zeno Regime ($\lambda_3 \approx 1.09$)**: Strong observation paradoxically slows down relaxation.

## Key Findings on Decoherence
Environmental noise **inverts the ordering** of the first two transitions ($\lambda_2$ now occurs before $\lambda_1$), demonstrating that decoherence fundamentally reorganizes the phase diagram of measurement.

## Relevance to `collapse`
- **Non-Gradual Transitions**: Suggests that "measurement-like" behavior may emerge abruptly as a function of coupling strength.
- **Decoherence Effects**: Warns that noise can decouple signatures that appear coincident in idealized models.
