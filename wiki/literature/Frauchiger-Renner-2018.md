# Frauchiger and Renner 2018 — Quantum theory cannot consistently describe the use of itself

**Reference**: Daniela Frauchiger and Renato Renner, 'Quantum theory cannot consistently describe the use of itself', Nature Communications 9, 3711 (2018). DOI: 10.1038/s41467-018-05739-8. arXiv: 1604.07422

## Core Thesis
Extended Wigner's friend scenario showing that if QM is universally valid, self-referential use leads to contradictions unless one gives up at least one of: single outcomes, universality of QM, or self-consistency of reasoning.

## Key Concepts
- Extended Wigner's friend scenario.
- No single-world interpretation of universally valid QM can consistently describe nested observers.

## Relevance to `unitary-collapse`
The Frauchiger–Renner argument is a useful consistency test for any eventual single-world interpretation. The current root construction does not yet resolve the paradox because it has not supplied a physical selection rule, observer reasoning framework, or verified single-outcome ontology.
