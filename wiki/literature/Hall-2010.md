# Hall 2010 — Local Deterministic Model of Singlet State Correlations

**Reference**: M. J. W. Hall, *Physical Review Letters* **105**(25), 250404 (2010). DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevLett.105.250404](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.105.250404). arXiv: [1007.5518](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.5518).

**Companion**: M. J. W. Hall, *Physical Review A* **84**(2), 022102 (2011). arXiv: [1102.4467](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.4467).

## Core Result
Hall proved exact quantitative bounds on the **minimal violation of Statistical Independence** needed to reproduce quantum correlations with a local deterministic model.

### Relaxed CHSH Inequality
Define the measurement dependence $M = \frac{1}{2} \sup_{a,b,a',b'} \int |\!P(\lambda \mid a,b) - P(\lambda \mid a',b')| \, d\lambda$. Then:

$$|S| \le 2 + 2M.$$

To reach the Tsirelson bound $S = 2\sqrt{2}$: $M \ge \sqrt{2} - 1 \approx 0.414$.

### Information Deficit
For uniform binary settings ($H(a,b) = 2$ bits), the mutual information required is:

$$I(\lambda : a,b) \ge 1 - h\!\left(\frac{1 + 1/\sqrt{2}}{2}\right) \approx 0.144 \text{ bits.}$$

**Remarkable consequence:** Less than 1/7 of a bit of correlation between source and settings suffices to explain the entire nonlocal phenomenology of the singlet state locally and deterministically.

## Key Contributions
- Explicit local deterministic models recovering $E(a,b) = -\cos(a-b)$ with no-signaling.
- Generalization to $N$-setting Bell and Kochen-Specker scenarios (2011 companion).
- Placed superdeterminism on a quantitative footing, replacing philosophical "conspiracy" debates with information-theoretic precision.

## Relevance to `unitary-collapse`
Hall's bounds establish that state-space restrictions need only carry a tiny amount of setting-correlated information to reproduce quantum statistics. If the `unitary-collapse` projective root distribution correlates initial states with the detector Hamiltonian (which determines measurement settings), the required "superdeterministic" information content may be negligibly small.
