# Aharonov et al. 1964 — Time Symmetry in the Quantum Process of Measurement

**Reference**: Y. Aharonov, P. G. Bergmann, and J. L. Lebowitz, 'Time Symmetry in the Quantum Process of Measurement', Physical Review 134(6B), B1410-B1416 (1964). DOI: 10.1103/PhysRev.134.B1410

## Core Thesis
Derives time-symmetric probability rule (ABL rule) for intermediate measurements given both initial and final states. Shows QM measurement is compatible with time-reversal symmetry given two-time boundary conditions.

## Key Concepts
- Time-symmetric probability rule for intermediate measurements (ABL rule).
- Quantum mechanics measurement is compatible with time-reversal symmetry given two-time boundary conditions.
- Key formula: P(b_m, t_m) = |<phi|U(T,t_m)|b_m><b_m|U(t_m,0)|psi>|^2 / sum over all outcomes

## Relevance to `unitary-collapse`
Two-time boundary conditions directly connect to Schulman's special states. The projective roots of unitary-collapse serve as dynamically determined 'final boundary conditions'.
