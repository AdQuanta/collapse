# Hardy 2001 — Quantum Theory From Five Reasonable Axioms

**Reference**: Lucien Hardy, 'Quantum Theory From Five Reasonable Axioms', arXiv: quant-ph/0101012 (2001).

## Core Thesis
Derives quantum theory from five physically motivated axioms about probability, including subspace simplicity, composite simplicity, and continuous reversibility. Classical probability theory satisfies the first four axioms; the fifth (continuous reversibility) singles out quantum theory.

## Key Concepts
- Derivation of quantum theory from five probability axioms.
- Axioms include subspace simplicity, composite simplicity, and continuous reversibility.
- Continuous reversibility singles out quantum theory from classical probability theory.

## Relevance to `unitary-collapse`
Information-theoretic reconstructions provide axiomatic reasons for why QM has its structure. If unitary-collapse can be connected to these axioms, the restricted-state hypothesis gains axiomatic support.
