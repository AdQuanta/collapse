# Hamid (2025): Emergence of Objective Classicality

## Core Thesis
Objective classicality (definite outcomes) can emerge from **purely unitary dynamics** without the need for the wave function collapse postulate.

## Key Mechanisms
- **Minimal Model Universe**: System ($\text{Q}$) $\to$ Environment ($\text{E}$) $\to$ Observer ($\text{O}$).
- **Nonlinear Amplification**: The observer is modeled as a particle in a double-well potential.
- **Necessary Conditions for Classicality**:
    1. **Large Environment**: Required for sufficient decoherence.
    2. **Observer-Environment Coupling ($\text{H}_{\text{EO}}$)**: The observer must be coupled to the environment to allow for redundant imprinting (Quantum Darwinism).
- **Symmetry Breaking**: Under these conditions, the observer's state undergoes spontaneous symmetry breaking and localizes in one well.

## Relevance to `collapse`
- **Unitary Loophole**: Directly supports the project's goal of deriving measurement-like outcomes from purely unitary evolution.
- **Environmental Redundancy**: Highlights the role of environmental imprinting in stabilizing macroscopic records.
