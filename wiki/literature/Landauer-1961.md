# Landauer 1961 — Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process

**Reference**: Rolf Landauer, 'Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process', IBM Journal of Research and Development 5(3), 183-191 (1961). DOI: 10.1147/rd.53.0183

## Core Thesis
Established that logical irreversibility (erasure of information) necessarily dissipates energy: erasing one bit costs at least kT ln 2 of heat. Information processing is physical and has thermodynamic consequences.

## Key Concepts
- Logical irreversibility (erasure of information) dissipates energy.
- Landauer's principle: 'Information is physical.'
- Erasing one bit costs at least kT ln 2 of heat.

## Relevance to `unitary-collapse`
Measurement produces records (new information), and record formation is thermodynamically irreversible. The measurement arrow of time is thus connected to the thermodynamic arrow through information theory.
