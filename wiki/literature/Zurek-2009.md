# Zurek 2009 — Quantum Darwinism

**Reference**: W. H. Zurek, *Nature Physics* **5**, 181–188 (2009).

## Core Thesis
Objectivity is an emergent property of how information about a quantum system is redundantly distributed in its environment. Classicality arises when multiple independent observers can reach a consensus about a system's state by intercepting disjoint fragments of the environment.

## Key Concepts
- **Environment as Witness**: The environment is treated as a medium that redundantly records information about the system, moving beyond the view of the environment as a passive "sink" for decoherence.
- **Redundancy as Objectivity**: A property is defined as "objective" if it is encoded redundantly across many environmental fragments. Only "pointer states" are capable of proliferating their information in this way.
- **Non-Invasive Observation**: Observers can identify the state by intercepting a small fraction of the environment, avoiding direct interaction with the system.
- **Symmetry Breaking**: Interaction with the environment breaks the egalitarian nature of quantum superpositions, promoting a small set of stable pointer states to classical objectivity.
