# Page and Wootters 1983 — Evolution without evolution

**Reference**: Don N. Page and William K. Wootters, 'Evolution without evolution: Dynamics described by stationary observables', Physical Review D 27(12), 2885-2892 (1983). DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevD.27.2885

## Core Thesis
In a static universe satisfying the Wheeler-DeWitt equation H|Psi>=0, time emerges from entanglement between a 'clock' subsystem C and the rest R. The conditional state of R given C's reading reproduces Schrödinger dynamics.

## Key Concepts
- Wheeler-DeWitt equation and static universe.
- Time emerges from entanglement between a 'clock' subsystem and the rest of the universe.
- Time itself is relational and emergent, not fundamental.

## Relevance to `unitary-collapse`
The Page-Wootters mechanism suggests that the measurement process and its arrow of time are emergent from the entanglement structure of the universe — consistent with unitary-collapse's view that collapse arises from the structure of the global state.
