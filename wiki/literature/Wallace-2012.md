# Wallace 2012 — The Emergent Multiverse

**Reference**: David Wallace, *The Emergent Multiverse: Quantum Theory according to the Everett Interpretation*, Oxford University Press, Oxford (2012). ISBN: 978-0199546961. DOI: [10.1093/acprof:oso/9780199546961.001.0001](https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780199546961.001.0001).

## Core Thesis
Worlds in the Many-Worlds Interpretation are not axiomatic primitive entities; they are **emergent macroscopic patterns** dynamically defined and decoupled by environmental decoherence (invoking Dennett's Criterion of Real Patterns). Wallace provides the definitive mathematical proof of the **Deutsch–Wallace Theorem**, which deduces the Born rule from the rational decision theory of an agent facing branching, without assuming stochasticity or non-unitary collapse.

## Key Mathematical & Conceptual Contributions
- **Decoherent Branching Structure:** Macroscopic branches form a tree structure defined by the history projectors of the pointer basis, with inter-branch cross-talk suppressed to $\mathcal{O}(e^{-S_{\text{env}}})$.
- **Deutsch–Wallace Theorem:** Wallace proves that any rational agent whose preference ordering $\succsim$ over quantum acts satisfies standard decision-theoretic axioms (Richness, State Supervenience, Solution Independence, and Branching Indifference) must order acts according to expected utility calculated with respect to Born weights:
  $$\mathcal{U}(\text{act}) = \sum_k |c_k|^2 u(x_k).$$
- **Resolution of the Probability Problem:** Rebuts both the *incoherence problem* (how can probability make sense in a deterministic universe?) and the *epistemic problem* (how can evidence confirm quantum mechanics?).

## Relevance to `unitary-collapse`
Wallace represents the strongest modern defense of no-collapse quantum mechanics via branching. While Wallace grounds the Born rule in the subjective rationality of an agent in a multiverse, `unitary-collapse` derives the Born rule physically and objectively from the algebraic density of projective roots in a single, unbranching universe.
