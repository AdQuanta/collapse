# Jarzynski 1997 — Nonequilibrium Equality for Free Energy Differences

**Reference**: Christopher Jarzynski, 'Nonequilibrium Equality for Free Energy Differences', Physical Review Letters 78(14), 2690-2693 (1997). DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.78.2690

## Core Thesis
Proved that the free energy difference between two equilibrium states can be computed from the work done in arbitrary non-equilibrium processes: <e^{-βW}> = e^{-βΔF}. Extends the second law to an equality relating fluctuations to equilibrium properties.

## Key Concepts
- Jarzynski equality relating non-equilibrium work to equilibrium free energy differences.
- Extends the second law to an equality relating fluctuations to equilibrium properties.
- Crooks fluctuation theorem.

## Relevance to `unitary-collapse`
Fluctuation theorems connect microscopic reversibility to macroscopic irreversibility quantitatively. The projective detailed balance condition in unitary-collapse has a structural parallel to the Crooks relation.
