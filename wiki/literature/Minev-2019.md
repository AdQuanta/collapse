# Minev et al. 2019 — To catch and reverse a quantum jump mid-flight

**Reference**: Z. K. Minev, S. O. Mundhada, S. Shankar, P. Reinhold, R. Gutiérrez-Jáuregui, R. J. Schoelkopf, M. Mirrahimi, H. J. Carmichael, and M. H. Devoret, 'To catch and reverse a quantum jump mid-flight', Nature 570, 200-204 (2019). DOI: 10.1038/s41586-019-1287-z

## Core Thesis
Demonstrated that quantum jumps in a superconducting qubit are not instantaneous and random but are continuous, coherent, deterministic processes that can be caught mid-flight and reversed.

## Key Concepts
- Quantum jumps are continuous and coherent, not instantaneous and random.
- The 'jump' has a predictable, deterministic trajectory that can be tracked via weak measurement and reversed by a control pulse.

## Relevance to `unitary-collapse`
Directly supports the unitary-collapse thesis: what appears as a discontinuous 'collapse' is actually a continuous unitary process. The quantum jump's deterministic trajectory is consistent with evolution through projective root states.
