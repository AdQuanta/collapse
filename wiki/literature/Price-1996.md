# Price 1996 — Time's Arrow and Archimedes' Point

**Reference**: Huw Price, 'Time's Arrow and Archimedes' Point: New Directions for the Physics of Time', Oxford University Press (1996). ISBN: 978-0195117981

## Core Thesis
Advocates viewing physics from a time-symmetric perspective ('Archimedean point'). The apparent arrow of time comes entirely from the Past Hypothesis (low-entropy initial condition), not from time-asymmetric laws.

## Key Concepts
- Time-symmetric perspective in physics.
- The apparent arrow of time originates from the Past Hypothesis.
- There is no intrinsic asymmetry in physics; intuitions about cause and effect are artifacts of the thermodynamic arrow.

## Relevance to `unitary-collapse`
Price's time-symmetric philosophy supports the unitary-collapse approach: if the laws are time-symmetric, then the measurement arrow must emerge from boundary conditions (initial state restrictions), exactly as projective roots provide.
