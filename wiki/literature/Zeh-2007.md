# Zeh 2007 — The Physical Basis of the Direction of Time

**Reference**: H. Dieter Zeh, *The Physical Basis of the Direction of Time*, 5th edition, Springer-Verlag, Berlin Heidelberg (2007). ISBN: 978-3-540-68001-7. DOI: [10.1007/978-3-540-68001-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-68001-7). (1st edition 1989; 2nd ed. 1992; 3rd ed. 1999; 4th ed. 2001).

## Core Thesis
The empirical arrows of time across physics—electrodynamics (retarded radiation), thermodynamics (entropy increase), quantum measurement (decoherence and collapse), gravitation (black holes and singularities), and cosmology (expansion)—are not independent laws of nature, but manifestations of a single, **master cosmological arrow of time** originating in an asymmetric initial cosmological boundary condition of extraordinarily low entropy and minimal entanglement.

## Key Concepts & Formalism
- **The Master Arrow of Time:** All physical arrows can be derived from the absence of initial microscopic correlations in the early universe.
- **Quantum Irreversibility as Decoherence:** The apparent collapse of the wave function in quantum measurement is a consequence of continuous, unavoidable entanglement with the unobserved macroscopic environment. Because the early universe had low entanglement, global unitary evolution naturally increases local entanglement entropy monotonically.
- **Improper Mixtures:** Zeh emphasizes that decoherence transforms pure states into *improper mixtures*, which look diagonal locally but remain pure global superpositions. Decoherence alone does not yield single outcomes without Many-Worlds or a collapse mechanism.
- **Timeless Quantum Cosmology:** In the Wheeler–DeWitt equation $\hat{\mathcal{H}}\Psi = 0$, external time does not exist. Time and its direction are emergent internal coordinates parameterized by physical degrees of freedom (e.g., scale factor $a$).
