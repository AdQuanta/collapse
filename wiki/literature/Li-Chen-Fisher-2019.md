# Li, Chen, and Fisher 2019 — Quantum Zeno effect and the many-body entanglement transition

**Reference**: Yaodong Li, Xiao Chen, and Matthew P. A. Fisher, 'Quantum Zeno effect and the many-body entanglement transition', Physical Review B 98(20), 205136 (2018). DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevB.98.205136. arXiv: 1808.06134. Also: Yaodong Li, Xiao Chen, and Matthew P. A. Fisher, 'Measurement-driven entanglement transition in hybrid quantum circuits', Physical Review B 100(13), 134306 (2019). DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevB.100.134306

## Core Thesis
Discovery that in random quantum circuits with projective measurements at rate p, a phase transition occurs at p=p_c between a volume-law entangled phase (p < p_c) and an area-law phase (p > p_c).

## Key Concepts
- Measurement-induced phase transitions (MIPT) in random quantum circuits.
- Transition between volume-law entangled phase and area-law phase driven by projective measurements.

## Relevance to `unitary-collapse`
MIPT demonstrates that measurement is a dynamical phase transition, not a passive observation. The transition has conceptual parallels to the scrambling-to-Born crossover predicted by unitary-collapse's spectral route.
