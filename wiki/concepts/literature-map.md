# Strategic Literature Map

This map organizes the foundational, comparative, and contextual literature for the `unitary-collapse` research program. In accordance with the **Scientific Writing Skill**, citations are treated as active pillars of the scientific argument, structured into four strategic functional sets:

$$\text{Foundations} \quad\big|\quad \text{Nearest Prior Art} \quad\big|\quad \text{Contrast \& Limitations} \quad\big|\quad \text{Implications \& Generalization}$$

---

## 1. Foundations (Field-Defining Papers & Authoritative Reviews)
*Works establishing the accepted baseline for quantum measurement, decoherence, closed-system thermalization, spectral graph theory, and informational axiomatics.*

- [[Born-1926]] — Max Born's foundational paper introducing the statistical interpretation of the wave function ($p \propto |\psi|^2$).
- [[Von-Neumann-1932]] — First rigorous mathematical axiomatization of QM; formal distinction between Process 1 (collapse) and Process 2 (unitary evolution).
- [[Everett-1957]] — Relative state formulation of quantum mechanics; eliminates Process 1 in favor of universal unitary evolution and branching.
- [[Erdos-Renyi-1959]] — Foundational paper introducing the theory of random graphs and the giant component phase transition.
- [[Chung-1997]] — Spectral Graph Theory: normalized Laplacians, Cheeger inequalities, and expander mixing.
- [[Abou-Chacra-Thouless-Anderson-1973]] — Exact analytical theory of Anderson localization on the Bethe lattice.
- [[Zurek-1982]] — Introduction of environment-induced superselection and pointer states.
- [[Zurek-2003]] — Comprehensive review of decoherence, einselection, and the quantum origins of the classical.
- [[Zurek-2009]] — Formulation of Quantum Darwinism (redundant recording of classical information).
- [[Joos-Zeh]] — Foundational continuous decoherence theory.
- [[Zeh-2007]] — The Physical Basis of the Direction of Time; all empirical arrows derived from a master cosmological boundary condition.
- [[Schlosshauer]] — Standard treatise on the decoherence program and its conceptual boundaries in measurement.
- [[Deutsch-1991]] — Formulation of the Eigenstate Thermalization Hypothesis (ETH) for closed quantum systems.
- [[Srednicki-1994]] — Extension of ETH to quantum chaos and subsystem thermalization under unitary dynamics.
- [[Gleason-1957]] — Gleason's theorem establishing the Born probability measure from frame functions in $\dim \mathcal{H} \ge 3$.
- [[Landauer-1961]] — Physical nature of information and the thermodynamic dissipation of bit erasure ($k_B T \ln 2$).
- [[Bohm-1952]] — David Bohm's foundational hidden-variable papers establishing deterministic pilot-wave dynamics (Parts I & II, 1952).
- [[DGZ-1992]] — Dürr, Goldstein & Zanghì's equivariance theorem, quantum equilibrium, and the typicality derivation of Born's rule in Bohmian mechanics.
- [[Hardy-2001]] — Quantum Theory From Five Reasonable Axioms; continuous reversibility singles out complex quantum mechanics.

## 2. Nearest Prior Art (Direct Conceptual & Methodological Precedents)
*Works exploring restricted initial state sets, emergent objectivity without wave-function collapse, quantum walks, or dynamic measurement transitions.*

- [[Schulman-Theory]] — L. S. Schulman's *Time's Arrows and Quantum Measurement* (1997, 2012, 2017), introducing "special states" that avoid macroscopic superpositions under unitary evolution.
- [[Farhi-Gutmann-1998]] — Continuous-time quantum walks on decision trees and general graphs.
- [[Caruso-et-al-2009]] — Environment-assisted quantum transport (ENAQT) on complex networks.
- [[Altshuler-et-al-1997]] — Quasiparticle lifetime and localization on high-dimensional Fock-space graphs.
- [[Aharonov-1964]] — Time symmetry in quantum measurement and the ABL rule for pre- and post-selected ensembles.
- [[Price-1996]] — Time's Arrow and Archimedes' Point; time-symmetric realism and the retrocausal resolution of Bell's theorem.
- [[Minev-2019]] — Experimental demonstration of catching and reversing a quantum jump mid-flight in superconducting qubits.
- [[Brandao-Piani-Horodecki-2015]] — Derivation of generic classicality and spectrum broadcast structures in many-body environments.
- [[Qi-Ranard-2021]] — Emergent classicality through quantum Markov blankets in isolated many-body systems.
- [[Hamid-2025]] — Observer-induced decoherence and the emergence of objective classicality from unitary dynamics.
- [[Guttel-2026]] — Dynamical measurement transitions in continuously monitored superconducting qubits.

## 3. Contrast & Limitations (The Conventional Barriers & Alternative Paradigms)
*Works defining the linearity obstruction, objective collapse modifications, no-go theorems, many-body localization, or alternative interpretations.*

- [[GRW-1986]] — Ghirardi-Rimini-Weber model of spontaneous wave-function localization via non-linear stochastic modifications.
- [[Pearle-1989-CSL]] — Continuous Spontaneous Localization (CSL) model: non-linear stochastic modification of the Schrödinger equation.
- [[Bassi-et-al-2013]] — Comprehensive review of objective collapse models, detailing experimental limits and theoretical requirements.
- [[Diosi-Penrose-Model]] — Gravity-induced spontaneous wave-function collapse mechanisms.
- [[Basko-Aleiner-Altshuler-2006]] — Many-Body Localization (MBL) as an Anderson localization transition in Fock space.
- [[Tikhonov-Mirlin-2016]] — Fractality of wave functions and non-ergodic extended states on random regular graphs.
- [[Wallace-2012]] — The Emergent Multiverse: modern defense of Many-Worlds and the decision-theoretic derivation of Born's rule.
- [[Griffiths-1984]] — Consistent histories interpretation: probability assignment to quantum history families without collapse.
- [[Gell-Mann-Hartle-1990]] — Decoherent histories for quantum cosmology and the emergence of quasiclassical realms.
- [[Fuchs-Mermin-Schack-2014]] — QBism: quantum states as personal Bayesian credences, dissolving the measurement problem by denying ontic states.
- [[Rovelli-1996]] — Relational Quantum Mechanics (RQM): physical variables take values only relative to other physical systems.
- [[Cramer-1986]] — Transactional Interpretation: time-symmetric standing-wave handshakes between retarded and advanced waves.
- [[Frauchiger-Renner-2018]] — Extended Wigner's friend no-go theorem: quantum theory cannot consistently describe itself.
- [[Hossenfelder-Palmer-2020]] — Comprehensive review of superdeterminism, non-computability, and constrained state spaces.
- [[Busch-2003]] — Extension of Gleason's theorem to generalized measurements (POVMs) and two-level systems (qubits).
- [[tHooft-2016]] — Gerard 't Hooft's Cellular Automaton Interpretation: Planck-scale deterministic permutation dynamics with quantum mechanics as emergent linear algebra.
- [[Hall-2010]] — M. J. W. Hall's quantitative bounds: only $\approx 0.14$ bits of measurement dependence saturates the Tsirelson bound.
- [[Conway-Kochen-2006]] — Conway & Kochen's Free Will Theorem: superdeterminism is the unique escape from the theorem's conclusion.
- [[Donadi-Hossenfelder-2022]] — Experimental proposals for testing superdeterministic memory timescales via rapid repeated measurements.

## 4. Implications & Generalization (Broader Physics & Mathematical Bridges)
*Works demonstrating the broader relevance to quantum chaos, complex networks, quantum thermodynamics, and non-ergodic dynamics.*

- [[Watts-Strogatz-1998]] — Small-world networks: collective dynamics, clustering, and short path lengths.
- [[Barabasi-Albert-1999]] — Emergence of scaling in random networks: preferential attachment and scale-free hubs.
- [[Childs-et-al-2003]] — Exponential algorithmic speedup by a quantum walk on glued-trees graphs.
- [[Kottos-Smilansky-1997]] — Quantum chaos on graphs: trace formulas and Wigner-Dyson spectral statistics.
- [[Mulken-Blumen-2011]] — Comprehensive review of continuous-time quantum walks across complex networks.
- [[Palmer-2026]] — Rational Quantum Mechanics, proposing a finite arithmetic restriction on physical state spaces ($N_{\max} < 1000$).
- [[Doucet-Deffner-2024]] — Microscopic Hamiltonian conditions required for the emergence of Quantum Darwinism.
- [[Popescu-Short-Winter-2006]] — Canonical typicality: why pure states in large environments appear locally canonical without ensemble averaging.
- [[Atas-et-al-2013]] — Level-spacing ratio distributions ($r$-statistics) for Wigner-Dyson vs. Poissonian spectral classes.
- [[Many-Body-Scars]] — Turner et al. (2018), Serbyn et al. (2021), Moudgalya et al. (2022) on non-thermalizing submanifolds in chaotic many-body systems.
- [[Barandes-2023]] — Barandes' Stochastic-Quantum Correspondence: quantum mechanics as an indivisible stochastic process on configuration space.
- [[Page-Wootters-1983]] — Evolution without evolution: emergence of time from subsystem entanglement in stationary global states.
- [[Jarzynski-1997]] — Non-equilibrium free energy equality: microscopic reversibility connecting work fluctuations to equilibrium free energy.
- [[Maccone-2009]] — Quantum solution to the arrow-of-time dilemma via memory erasure in anti-thermodynamic evolutions.
- [[Li-Chen-Fisher-2019]] — Measurement-induced entanglement phase transitions in monitored hybrid quantum circuits.
