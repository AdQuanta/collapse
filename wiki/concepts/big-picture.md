# The Big Picture: Unitary Collapse

## The Fundamental Question
Can measurement-like collapse and Born-like outcome statistics arise from **pure unitary dynamics** of a qubit coupled to a complex detector?

## The "Loophole"
The standard objection to this idea is the linearity of quantum mechanics: if all superpositions in Hilbert space are physically realized, unitary evolution of outcome-producing states would generate macroscopic superpositions.

The project explores a specific loophole: **Allow the physically realized set of states/trajectories to be a restricted subset of Hilbert space that need not be closed under superposition.**

## Conceptual Alignment
This approach is related to:
- **Schulman's "Special States"**: The idea that only a subset of states is physically accessible.
- **Superselection**: The existence of superselection rules that forbid certain superpositions.
- **Emergent Classicality**: How a classical-looking world emerges from a quantum substrate.

## Highest Priority Objective
The current goal is to find structural conditions on the Hamiltonian/relative propagator that distinguish a **Born-like special-state point process** from:
1. **Haar/Uniform controls**: Maximally random systems.
2. **Strict-QND/No-cloud controls**: Systems that don't produce the necessary root geometry.
