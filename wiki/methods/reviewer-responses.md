# Reviewer Response Protocol

The goal of a reviewer response is to resolve scientific misunderstandings while maintaining a generous and exact tone.

## The Response Loop
For every reviewer comment, the following loop is completed:
`acknowledge` $\to$ `answer scientifically` $\to$ `make/justify change` $\to$ `identify modified text/figure` $\to$ `cite evidence`.

## Handling Disagreements
When a reviewer's claim conflicts with the evidence:
1. **Appreciation**: Acknowledge the reviewer's perspective.
2. **Shared Ground**: Find the area where both parties agree.
3. **Evidence**: Present the specific data, theorem, or control that supports the current result.
4. **Narrow Disagreement**: Define exactly where the disagreement lies.
5. **Clarification**: Update the manuscript to make the point more obvious, treating the reviewer's confusion as a clarity issue.

## General Guidelines
- **Tone**: Be generous in tone but exact in substance.
- **Transparency**: Do not minimize substantial changes; show the revised paragraph or figure.
- **Referencing**: Hyperlink cited papers by DOI in the response to facilitate referee review.
- **Consistency**: Use one consistent term (`reviewer` or `referee`) throughout the document.
