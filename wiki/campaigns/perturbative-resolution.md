# Perturbative Born claim: audited status

**OPEN.** The earlier positive-resolution and open-phase claims are withdrawn.
The [claim audit](../../research_reports/BORN_PHASE_AUDIT_2026-09-12.md)
separates valid measure identities from unproved microscopic assertions.

**PROVED, conditional on a limiting law and nondegenerate coverage:** exact
Born balance is equivalent to reflection invariance of
sin²(theta/2) mu, or all of its odd cosine moments vanishing. This is a
condition on a measure, not an existence theorem for the Hamiltonian limit.
The north-pole atom satisfies the moment constraints vacuously.

**FALSIFIED as written:** the previous potential formula p=J'. The first
derivative is the log-radius CDF; the second distributional derivative is
the interior measure. For a density the correct relation uses J''.
Endpoint masses are encoded by the limiting slopes.

**PROVED in the existing Theorem C:** no central-X field interval has the
Born limit within the X-conserving class, even with an interacting detector.
Consequently an open phase in the full admissible parameter space cannot
contain an X-conserving seed. Genuinely multichannel regions remain open
questions. See [[asymptotic-obstructions]].

positive-seed snapshots. At largest N, normalized binned detailed-balance
residuals are .05808 and .05675 for the two X-only rings, .27107 for config
079 and .36732 for config 047. All are single-time observations with missing
fresh QZ certification and no generic perturbation validation.
