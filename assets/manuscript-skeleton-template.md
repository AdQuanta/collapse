# High-impact scientific manuscript skeleton

## One-sentence pitch

[We solve / reveal / demonstrate X, which resolves Y and matters because Z.]

## Abstract logic

1. Context:
2. Gap:
3. Here we:
4. Result 1:
5. Result 2:
6. Significance/outlook:

## Introduction

### Paragraph 1 — Territory and importance
[Why should a broad expert care?]

### Paragraph 2 — State of the art
[What is known and what current approaches achieve?]

### Paragraph 3 — Gap / unresolved limitation
[What exactly remains missing? Why does it matter?]

### Paragraph 4 — Here we
[What this paper does; direct novelty statement.]

### Paragraph 5 — Principal findings / intuition
[The main results and the conceptual mechanism, without turning this into a section roadmap.]

## Discussion

1. Generalization beyond the exact system.
2. Most important conceptual implication.
3. Limitations / boundary of the claim.
4. Experimental, theoretical, or technological opportunities.
5. Strongest justified final outlook.

## Supplementary information plan

- detailed methods;
- derivations;
- controls/convergence;
- additional data;
- robustness tests;
- notation and acronym definitions;
- supporting figures that do not carry the headline claim.
