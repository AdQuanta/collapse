## Figure audit

For every figure, verify:
- one dominant idea;
- obvious reading order;
- strongest panel emphasized;
- comparison visually immediate;
- meaningful axis labels and units;
- readable final-size font;
- consistent color semantics;
- no unnecessary gridlines/noise;
- caption starts with an informative title;
- vector export when appropriate.
