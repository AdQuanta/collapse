# Results needed before submission

This registry contains only missing work that would change a manuscript claim or submission readiness. It distinguishes the exact antipodal root theorem from the still-missing detector-vector, numerical-validation, and physical-selection results.

## Claims that remain intentionally excluded

- A universal derivation of definite outcomes.
- A universal Born rule for generic structured detectors.
- A transverse-field Born-to-uniform crossover.
- Equivalence between Born-like roots and chaos, Poisson statistics, Wigner-Dyson statistics, or many-body localization.
- Density of the special set in the thermodynamic limit.
- A fundamental quantum-computing capacity bound.

These claims may be reopened only after the specific evidence above exists; wording changes alone cannot remove the gaps.
