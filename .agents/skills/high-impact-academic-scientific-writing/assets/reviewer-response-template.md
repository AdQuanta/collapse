## Rebuttal audit

Before submission, verify:
- every substantive comment has a direct answer;
- every answer is evidence-based;
- the revised manuscript contains a concrete change when feasible;
- disagreements are respectful and precise;
- relevant literature raised by reviewers is considered for citation;
- new revisions are clearly distinguishable from text that already existed;
- terminology uses either “reviewer” or “referee” consistently.
